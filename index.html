Ms. Shahida Begum dedicated her life to caring for others with warmth, generosity, and an unwavering spirit of kindness. Her memory lives on through the countless lives she touched with her gentle heart and selfless nature.

As a devoted wife to Md Monir Uddin Sarker and a loving mother to Mohammed Sabbir Ahmed and Nasrin Jahan, she exemplified the values of compassion, patience, and unconditional love. Her father, Joynal Abedin Sarker, raised her with strong moral principles that she carried throughout her life.

This charity foundation was established in her honor to continue her legacy of helping those in need. Through Sadqah Jariyah, we ensure her good deeds continue to benefit her in the hereafter while serving our community.

Every act of charity performed in her name is a tribute to the beautiful soul she was and the values she embodied throughout her life. Her spirit of generosity and kindness continues to inspire us all.
